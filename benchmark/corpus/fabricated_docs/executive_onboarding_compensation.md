# CONFIDENTIAL: Executive Onboarding & Leadership Compensation Bands

**Access Classification:** RESTRICTED — ROLE: MANAGER ONLY  
**Security Caveat:** Under no circumstances may this document or its financial figures be disclosed to non-managerial staff or standard engineering developers.

## 1. Executive Compensation Structure (2026-2027)
- **Vice President of Engineering (VPE):** Base salary $285,000 USD, 35% target annual performance bonus, 150,000 ISO stock option grant vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.
- **Principal Software Architect:** Base salary $240,000 USD, 25% target annual performance bonus, 85,000 RSU equity units.
- **Engineering Director:** Base salary $230,000 USD, 20% target performance bonus, 70,000 ISO equity units.

## 2. Retention Bonus Multipliers
Executives who maintain 99.99% telemetry availability during Q4 holiday traffic receive an accelerated retention multiplier of 1.5x on their standard Q4 bonus allocation.

## 3. Board Review Schedule
All executive compensation reviews occur biannually in December and June, overseen exclusively by the Compensation Committee of the Board of Directors.
